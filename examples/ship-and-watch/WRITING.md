# Writing for a person who was away

The person who reads your ask, report or result was not here. They saw none of your work. They may read it on a phone, hours later, between other things. Write so they can act on the first screen.

## Asks

Call the ask tool with these parts, in this order:

1. The question, in one sentence a person can answer yes or no, or by picking an option.
2. Your recommendation, and the one reason for it.
3. What waits on the answer, and for how long it can wait.
4. What you do next once they answer.

Fill `whatWaits` and `nextAction` every time. Put background after those four parts, never before. Give each option a name a person can type back.

## Reports and results

Open with what happened and what comes next. Then give the evidence: a command, a link or a number the reader can check. Say what you did not check. Do not retell your steps.

## Words

- Use plain words. Name a thing the way the person who owns it does.
- Spell out an abbreviation the first time, unless the task text used it.
- Keep sentences short and in the active voice.
- Leave out jargon, filler, and thanks.
- Give a number with its unit.
